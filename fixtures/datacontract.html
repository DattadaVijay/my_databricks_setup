Terms of Use

High level description of the dataset including purpose, usage guidelines, and limitations

Purpose
Govern customer profiles, banking transactions, fraud labels, historical behavioral features, and machine learning training datasets across the Databricks medallion architecture.
Usage
Supports fraud analytics, point-in-time feature engineering, model training, batch inference, and online risk scoring.
Limitations
Fraud labels are investigation outcomes, not definitive legal findings. Feature aggregates depend on the completeness of upstream transactions. Currency and source timezone are not explicitly defined in the current source schema.
